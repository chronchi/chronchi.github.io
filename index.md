# Carlos Ronchi

I am a Scientist II at [Tempus AI](https://www.tempus.com) in Chicago, working at
the intersection of computational biology and pharma R&D. I'm a
mathematician by training, and I build rigorous quantitative methods from
first principles. At Tempus I now work on agentic AI workflows and multimodal data
analysis.

Before Tempus I completed a PhD in molecular life sciences at
[EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch) in the
[Brisken lab](https://brisken-lab.epfl.ch), studying estrogen-receptor-positive
breast cancer. I hold an MSc in Mathematics from the
[Universidade de São Paulo](http://www5.usp.br/english/?lang=en), where I
worked on topological data analysis and protein stability under
[Dr. Marcio Gameiro](http://www.icmc.usp.br/pessoas/gameiro/); and a BSc in
Mathematics from the Federal University of Paraná (UFPR).
 
## Research interests

My research interests are in computational biology,
bayesian statistics, topological data analysis, machine learning and agentic workflows. 
I'm also very interested in science epistemology and how to 
encode and reason about uncertainty in science.
